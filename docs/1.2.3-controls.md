# UwU Dimension 1.2.3 — music, guardian and acquisition controls

Minecraft1.21.1 / NeoForge21.1.249. Replaces the previous UwU JAR; keep only one UwU version. No world, installed profile, songpack or other mod was changed during development.

## Cherry-night music
Optional compatibility for Reactive Music1.3.5 treats `uwudimension:heartwater`, `uwudimension:blossom_reach` and `uwudimension:sakura_reach` as Overworld **only inside its music event sampler**. Actual world IDs, biomes, travel and generation remain unchanged. This also gives the music sampler normal Overworld underground/high-altitude conditions. Reactive Music remains optional.

The supplied ZIP filename says2.2.2 but its internal songpack version is2.3.6. Its cherry-night rule requires NIGHT, DIM=OVERWORLD and one of the named cherry/aspen biomes, selecting `river_night`. The existing pack still decides priority, timing and audio. No audio is included or redistributed. Daytime cherry uses the existing floral rule; underwater uses the pack's water rule unless an earlier higher-priority rule wins, exactly as in the Overworld. This is not a replacement songpack.

## UwU Guardian
Default translated name: **UwU Guardian**. Registry/save ID stays `uwudimension:blossom_warden`; custom name tags remain intact.

Maximum health is exactly6666 health points (3333 hearts). Only a guardian-owned maximum-health attribute instance bypasses vanilla's1024 clamp; other mobs and the global attribute range are unchanged. New guardians begin full. Older guardians migrate once at their existing health percentage, e.g.160/320 becomes3333/6666. Later saves preserve damage normally; the update never fills health every tick.

Regeneration IV is maintained while ticking, including after effects are removed. Speed IV is applied only during pursuit/combat, and the guardian's short-lived speed effect is removed when idle. Stronger outside effects are preserved. Existing proactive village defense,30-second blocked pursuit interception and real8000-block throw remain intact.

## Cupid hearts
Cupid's arrow trail, successful charm burst and recurring hearts around the charmed target now use an original soft pastel-pink heart particle. The recurring particles stop when charm expires. Other sources and vanilla heart textures remain unchanged. This does not expand which entities Cupid can charm or change its40-second duration.

## Two server controls
File: `<world>/serverconfig/uwudimension-server.toml` (single player: `<profile>/saves/<world>/serverconfig/uwudimension-server.toml`). Both defaults are true:

```toml
heartLocketCraftingEnabled = true
weaponAcquisitionEnabled = true
```

Stop the world/server, edit these values, then restart. They are marked world-restart settings; changing a file or running `/reload` in an already-running world does not switch policy mid-crafting/menu. NeoForge supplies the server policy to connected clients. For newly created worlds, a pack can supply the same file in `defaultconfigs`.

`heartLocketCraftingEnabled=false` prevents the heartwater locket recipe from matching, including manual/shift crafting, and removes its recipe from the server's synchronized recipe collection. Existing lockets still work.

`weaponAcquisitionEnabled=false` blocks new survival weapon outputs through recipe lookup, villager trade result slots and loot generation, including the final result after global loot modifiers. All149 weapons remain registered. Native UwU chest loot and Lootr first-time per-player rolls obey the setting, even if the chest block existed before the setting changed. The two controls are independent; disabling weapons does not disable locket crafting.

Existing owned items remain usable, transferable and recoverable after dropping. Previously opened per-player Lootr inventories are preserved. Already-materialized raw chest inventories/ground drops cannot reliably be distinguished from player-stored belongings and are not erased. Creative/operator grants remain available. The setting is an acquisition policy, not an inventory ban. Visual scythe/glitch projectile stacks never become pickup rewards or generate replacement items.

## Future companion integration
Public API: `dev.uwu.dimension.AcquisitionPolicy.heartLocketCraftingEnabled()`, `weaponAcquisitionEnabled()`, `isWeapon(ItemStack)`, `allowsWeapon(ItemStack)` and `allowsCraft(ItemStack)`. `SPEC` and the two BooleanValue fields expose the same NeoForge server settings. Companion mods should consult the getters at their own reward/crafting boundaries and respect world-restart semantics. No Unyielding JAR was modified.

Validation results and exact artifact hash accompany the final package. Hosted matcher tests do not establish that the private recording was audibly played on the user's machine.

Named relic audit
All 15 named relics below are retained in Combat creative and the four weapon chest pools; each remains a possible Glitch Bow form. Their listed hints describe existing intended effects, not independently verified gameplay. Stable registered IDs are kept.

ID | Display name | Damage | Attack speed | Ability cooldown (ticks) | Source hint

ripple_bow | Rippleglass Bow | 17 | 1.25 | 24 | Draw and release. On impact, a ripple marks and slows nearby creatures.
ribbonhare_scepter | Ribbonhare Scepter | 14 | 1.6 | 200 | Send a glowing pink orb to solid ground to call a pink hare. Its thumps draw monsters.
blossom_greatsword | Blossom Great Axe | 30 | 0.85 | 44 | Throw the blossom axe for a ranged strike. Fully charged melee hits still scatter petals.

Also retained: glitch_bow — Combat creative and all four weapon chest pools; its 15 named and 133 counter numeric form indices remain in their original order. New random counter forms use the 32 direct representatives; existing selected counter forms retain their original IDs.
heart_launcher — Combat creative and recipe data/uwudimension/recipe/heart_launcher.json; launches heart shots, 12-tick cooldown, terrain excavation disabled by default.
bell_hammer — Tools creative and recipe data/uwudimension/recipe/bell_hammer.json; deliberate crouch mining can queue a 9 by 9 plane when enabled.
Named relics are not consolidated merely because they share a model shape: e.g. Bellheart pulls, Lockkeeper pushes, Ribbonhare summons, and Twinfin summons two axolotls. The legacy registered ID blossom_greatsword is retained while its in-game item is a Blossom Great Axe.

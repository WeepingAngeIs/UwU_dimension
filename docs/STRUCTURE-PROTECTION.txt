UwU Dimension -- authored structure protection

Scope: newly accepted ordinary Minecraft StructureStarts in Sakura Reach.
Other dimensions and the existing uwudimension:replaced_villages compatibility tag policy are unchanged.

Reservations come from a read-only mode of the CURRENT actual builders, not an independently guessed village-radius list. The planner records the surface settlement (all houses/styles, roads, gates, plaza and furnishings), conditional Moonveil treehouse and VillageSakura landmark, spring/bank, pools and boardwalks, deep lakes and connecting passages/stairs, bathhouse, archive, Mirror Court, descent and conditional hanging shrine. The same site eligibility, settlement lattice, historical snapshots, rotations and authored template geometry are reused. Unrelated ambient vegetation and the natural giant-sakura forest are not treated as buildings.

The planning mode never writes blocks, spawns residents, requests live/generating chunks or reads neighboring blocks. Native noise/base-height surveys supply ground heights. Potential clearances extend to the build ceiling; village foundation columns reserve down to the build floor. These are intentionally conservative rectangular envelopes including cleared air and foundations, rather than just occupied blocks. Distinct disconnected landmarks keep separate envelopes. A foreign building inside cleared space is still a collision.

Before StructureManager commits a new valid start, its complete adjusted bounding box is checked against all potential site cells it can reach. The candidate's origin chunk need not be near the authored footprint. An overlapping start is stored as INVALID_START before references or pieces are placed. No overlapping blocks are erased, clipped or repaired after placement. Nonintersecting ordinary starts within the survey budget are accepted normally, regardless of mod namespace.

Work is bounded: each site is within 144 blocks of its 768-block cell anchor; the reviewed local authored geometry is bounded to +/-256. Queries expand by 400 blocks and survey at most 256 cells. Derived per-level footprint caches are capped around 512 cells and contain only names/boxes, not saved gameplay state. The existing bounded native land survey caches remain in use. Cache eviction cannot change eligibility. No new saved-data schema or world migration is introduced.

IMPORTANT LIMITS
- A start requiring more than 256 candidate cells is conservatively declined with a bounded, per-registry-ID warning. It is not proved overlapping. Thus acceptance of every possible gigantic nonintersecting mod structure is NOT guaranteed. A concrete next step is to profile that named generator and add an indexed/hierarchical reservation query or a carefully tested larger budget; do not remove the bound or silently accept an unverified huge footprint.
- Arbitrary features, direct block writers, manual /place operations that bypass registration, and mods that expand pieces or write outside their declared start bounding boxes are not generally protected. Direct-write bypass is demonstrated in the disposable fixture. Supporting a specific such generator requires an atomic preflight adapter using its complete plan before any writes. Rejecting individual setBlock calls would cut buildings apart and is intentionally avoided.
- Already-saved starts are left intact, including pre-fix partially generated foreign structures. There is no retroactive repair, removal or retrogen. An old saved foreign start can still overlap later-generated authored parts. New starts accepted under this revision retain the same decisions over restart/partial placement while the seed, generator/datapacks, settlement snapshots, world border and relevant settings remain unchanged. Changing those inputs across versions is outside that guarantee.
- The full installed modpack was not run. STRUCTURE-REGISTRY-AUDIT.json records read-only installed-JAR declarations and checksums, not proof of which registry ID appears in the screenshot. In particular Ars Additions declares vanilla jigsaw buildings, When Dungeons Arise declares vanilla jigsaw structures, Ars Nouveau declares custom den structures, and Dungeon Crawl declares its custom structure type. Ordinary complete starts share the same commit hook; that is not proof that every mod's processors or later feature writes honor its bounds.

Hosted fixtures use three seeds, complete current authored plan envelopes, inclusive boundaries, a foreign origin 30 chunks away, a 3000-block-wide footprint, an oversized bounded rejection, normal nonoverlap acceptance/actual marker placement, both factions and four geometry rotations, conditional landmark geometry, reverse order after actual village generation, another-dimension acceptance, the existing replaced-village tag, native saved start/piece loading and restart after partial placement. Native village bells must survive. The separate achievement job exercises the existing 50-entry tree and saved partial/earned progress. Synthetic geometry tests exercise builders directly and do not assert a natural plot is eligible in every seed. These tests are separate from full-pack client or live-server play.

Cupid input/charm/wish mechanics, achievements, lore-book acquisition/text/art, saved-data code, all assets and all unrelated changes remain outside this patch.

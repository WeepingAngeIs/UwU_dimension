# Glitch form lifecycle and compatibility

The saved `uwu_form` index continues to address the original 148-entry list. A stack with no form displays and initializes index zero (Cupid Bow). Base damage and speed are calculated from the active form through the NeoForge item-attribute event; existing item component patches are not rewritten. Form-specific use, draw animation, existing melee hooks, and tool actions dispatch against the current form. The original owned Glitch stack is reserved during a physical throw and returned by `RelicReturns`.

After a successful ability, the stack remains pending until its applicable cooldown and ability complete. Counter forms also remain pending for the full 160-tick matching ward, which is longer than their item cooldown. Thrown stacks cannot advance while held in return custody. Bow arrows, whips and scythe waves carry unique action tokens; their actual destruction writes a durable completion receipt with any known lasting-effect deadline to overworld saved data. Physical projectiles update the reserved item’s deadline when their impact applies a lasting effect. Unloaded projectiles do not count as completed. Fishing forms track the original hook owner and hook UUID, wait for that hook to finish even if the stack is transferred, and allow its owner to reel while pending. If the original owner is offline while somebody else holds the stack, the form conservatively remains pending until that owner reconnects. The pending state follows the stack when moved or saved. Missing receipts do not guess completion or change the form.

Each used stack gets a random persistent identity. Up to 64 distinct action UUID receipts are retained per identity. This bounds normal per-shot history, but a capped record remains in world data for each distinct used stack, even after the item is destroyed. Deleting a receipt merely because the current inventory has advanced is not proven crash-safe: player, chest, entity and world-data saves are independent. A copied/restored pending stack whose shared identity has accumulated more than 64 later completed actions on other copies may remain pending because its receipt was evicted; distinct action UUIDs ensure it cannot be falsely completed by another action. There is no automatic recovery for this rare stale-copy case.

An optional `@Pseudo` mixin asks Better Combat for the selected form's attributes using a temporary form stack, then uses a shape fallback if the form has no Better Combat registration. It never writes a Better Combat preset into the real stack; an explicitly supplied preset retains precedence. Better Combat is optional. This describes intended compatibility, and actual behavior must be judged against hosted integration tests.

Successful Cupid charm waits at least 800 ticks from impact; Twinfin waits until both summoned companions are removed; Frost tracks its actual freeze and slowdown; Rift marks last up to 160 ticks; counter marks wait at least 160 ticks from impact; Ripple tracks every affected mob’s glow and slowdown; Blue Hour tracks its affected target; Ribbonhare waits for the summoned hare to be removed; and scythe landing charms wait at least 200 ticks. A second sneak-use of Ribbonstep is allowed once its normal cooldown ends even while its mark remains active. These are conservative deadlines: the form may remain longer if a target dies, an effect is cleared or a summon disappears early. An empty Blue Hour cast has no affected-entity hold and waits its normal cooldown. Affected target UUIDs are retained for active charms, marks, vanilla status effects, freeze/fire and summoned companions. Checks inspect already-loaded entities only and never load their chunks: unloaded or offline recipients leave the form pending. Confirmed destructive entity removal marks tracked recipients resolved; dimension changes and chunk unload do not. A missing recipient with no recorded destructive removal remains pending conservatively until it returns, rather than guessing completion. The projectile, summon/mark deadlines, custody, ward and cooldown constraints are checked independently; an unresolved projectile never counts as complete.

Affected-entity backreferences retain up to 64 simultaneous action links per entity. If more than 64 overlapping cloned/independent actions target the same entity, a very old pending action may remain stuck after that entity is destroyed because its backreference was evicted; this fails closed and does not cause an early switch. Receipt and entity/player/chunk saves are not atomic across an abrupt crash, so missing completion data can also leave a form pending rather than duplicating the weapon or guessing completion.

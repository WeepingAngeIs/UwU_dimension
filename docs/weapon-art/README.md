# Weapon sprite provenance and rebuild

The 153 item PNGs listed in `manifest.json` are original sprites created for this project in November 2026. Their designs were generated with OpenAI image generation from project-owned references; no Simply Swords or other third-party texture was copied. Generated transparent concept sheets were cropped into individual cells, converted to 32×32, and reviewed at enlarged nearest-neighbor scale. Generated art is interpreted and rasterized; it is not a claim of hand-drawn pixel art.

`weapon-sprite-atlas.png` is the exact, lossless pixel source for the final 153 textures. The manifest maps each item ID to its atlas cell, output texture path, design-family label, and SHA-256 of its 32×32 RGBA pixels. Counter entries also include their gameplay roster shape, theme, school, and target. The native atlas is included rather than the larger generated concept sheets. The labels are provenance labels, not files needed by the rebuild. `rebuild_sprites.py` requires Python 3 and Pillow; run `python docs/weapon-art/rebuild_sprites.py --output <new-output-folder>` to reconstruct and verify the 153 exact RGBA pixel arrays. Use a separate output folder when checking; compare with the paths in the manifest. PNG encoding bytes may vary by Pillow version even where pixels match.

The refreshed set covers all 133 retunable counter items and 20 individual named weapon/tool textures, including the four Vesper forms, Glitch Bow, Heart Launcher, Bell Hammer, and the single-headed Roseglass Axe. Multiple material/shape families intentionally reuse the same art for closely related retuned variants. The 32 directly obtainable representatives receive distinct art within their categories; distinct IDs do not all imply distinct imagery.

Intentionally preserved: every Cupid Bow texture and model, including its draw stages, as specifically requested; Rippleglass Bow and all its draw-stage textures and models, because generated replacements did not reliably preserve clear drawing progression; Glitch Bow's separate static/noise texture and all existing form models/overrides, to preserve its glitch animation and form identity; aura overlays and all item model transforms and predicates. No gameplay code, mechanics, particles, or projectile models were changed by the artwork pass. The high-resolution generated concept sheets are not packaged; they are not needed to reconstruct the finished textures from the included native atlas.

Validation performed: all 153 outputs are 32×32 with transparent and opaque pixels; the atlas rebuild verifies every pixel checksum; all 197 `counter_*.json` item models had their explicit UwU texture references checked for existence. This is asset validation, not proof of in-game appearance or animation behavior.

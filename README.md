# UwU Dimension 1.2

The current release adds the ranged counter arsenal, animated whips, elemental scythes, Glitch Bow, rare dimension-only loot and discoverable Mirror Court. Start with [the 1.2 guide](ARSENAL-GUIDE.md) and [the audited counter roster](COUNTER-ROSTER.md).

A standalone adventure mod for Minecraft Java 1.21.1. Flowering cliffs surround Heartwater, the first valley. Behind its waterfalls, a lamp-lit boardwalk leads to an underground bathhouse where three towels still wait for guests.

## Install and travel

Use Java 21 and NeoForge 21.1.249. Place **UwU-Dimension-1.21.1-1.2.5.jar** in the profile's `mods` folder on the client and server. Lootr, Another Furniture 4.0.2 and Simply Swords 1.70.2-1.21.1 are required on both the client and server. Keep Simply Swords' Architectury/Fzzy Config dependencies and its client Simply Tooltips dependency from the existing pack. Cobblemon enables the Pokémon habitats. The target is Cobblemon Unyielding 1.0.10 with Lootr 1.11.38.125, Cobblemon 1.8.4+1.21.1 and Kotlin for Forge 5.12.0. The existing profile supplies these dependencies. For a fresh standalone installation, use the same versions and add the furniture mod as its own JAR. [Another Furniture 4.0.2 for NeoForge 1.21.1](https://modrinth.com/mod/another-furniture/version/Q29JlZfU)

Pick up a small flower to unlock the Heartwater Locket recipe. Craft **one compass, one amethyst shard and one small flower** anywhere in the crafting grid. Right-click the locket to travel. Carry the same locket and right-click it again to return to the place where you entered. A fresh unbound locket used inside takes you back to Overworld spawn.

### Upgrading an existing world

Close the game and stop the server before replacing the old UwU Dimension JAR. Keep a backup of the world. Keep only one enabled UwU Dimension JAR per installation. Open your existing save and use your existing locket normally from the Overworld to visit **Blossom Reach**, the newly generated dimension. If you logged out in old Heartwater, first use your locket to return home, then use it again after the cooldown.

To revisit your original builds, **crouch and use the locket from the Overworld**. Old Heartwater keeps its original dimension ID, explored chunks, chests and residents. Terrain defects already saved in those chunks are preserved along with player builds. Use the locket normally in either realm to return. You can keep exploring without deleting dimension folders or resetting a save.

If you played the early 1.1.0 test JAR, normal locket use now takes you to a separate final Reach with the Japanese villages. To revisit the early test realm, hold **pink petals in your off hand**, crouch and use the locket from the Overworld. Use the locket normally there to return. Your early test chunks stay in their original dimension.

At the arrival meadow, face the waterfalls. Follow the stepping stones through the middle curtain. Descend the mossy stairs and stay on the cherry boardwalk to reach the pink-tiled bathhouse. Open the golden Lootr chest beyond the towels. Every player can claim a Ribbonhare Scepter and another relic there. Aim at a solid ledge and use the scepter. The rabbit's thumps draw nearby monsters before it hops back. Take it to a deep lake. One gentle creature in each pool can answer the thump.

## Along the water

- Turn east from the first lake to reach darker water, luminous shores and the lower stairs. Look up across the bell lake for an inverted shrine. Its upright stair and level gallery lead to the reward.

- Leave the bell lake by the descending western passage for the drowned archive. The shrine and archive have different reward pools. Further valleys rotate, and shrines occur in roughly a quarter of them.

- Pink-roofed homes belong to Petal Keepers. Look for mulberry-pink roofs and dark posts at the Dusk settlement. Show a Dusk Keeper an emerald in either hand and interact to trade. Showing money gives you a 20-second grace period. Each faction has four offers, including a way to earn emeralds.

- To choose a faction and recruit, crouch and interact with four emeralds in hand. The first oath locks in that player's side. Crouch and interact with an empty hand to settle a recruit or ask them to follow again.

- Cobblemon has 27 added spawn entries through its native player spawner. Flower fields, settlement outskirts, upper caves, deep stone and submerged waterways have their own eligibility rules. Each entry limits spawning to the intended UwU realms. Server spawn caps, cooldowns and bucket weights still apply.

## Villages, reefs and the Mirror Court

On arrival the locket announces the coordinates of the Petal village bell. Follow branching stone and earth paths past lanterns and timber gates. Four different house exteriors adapt individual buildings from the supplied Japanese village schematic. Look for white plaster panels between exposed posts and verandas beneath deep, layered eaves. The projecting roof courses, lifted ends and contrasting capped ridges draw on Boulevardier's Japanese Chateau. Bright Sakura and deeper Mulberry ceramic tiles run over solid pink concrete and terracotta. Each house sits on its own supported floor and connects to the street at its working door. Adult keepers use beds and workstations. To heal a wounded friendly keeper, hold food and interact. For a Dusk Keeper, showing an emerald in the other hand can keep the interaction friendly. A meal restores health based on its nutrition.

Stained glass brings daylight into the homes through the back wall and roof. Petal homes use pink, white and light blue glass, while Dusk homes use magenta, white and purple. Inside, look for a reading sofa between tall bookcases, a workshop with a crafting bench and tool shelves, a bamboo dining alcove with cake, or a garden bench between potted azaleas. Each home also has a tea table and an opening drawer. Underground, the bathhouse has poolside benches and the archive has a lamplit reading table.

**Teaching a profession.** Place an unclaimed workstation within 12 blocks of an unemployed adult, with a walkable route to it. Hold another block of the same workstation type and interact normally with that keeper. You keep the block. A Dusk visitor can show an emerald in the other hand. For example, a composter teaches farming, a lectern teaches librarianship and a smithing table teaches toolsmithing. Keepers can also find their own jobs. Trading locks in the profession. Their job adds ordinary profession offers to the faction offers, and working at their station lets them restock. Children grow up before taking jobs.

A flower guardian pursues whoever hurts it, including hostile mobs. If it cannot reach its attacker within about 30 seconds, it closes the distance, grabs them and launches them roughly 8,000 blocks upward. Take care when aiming near one.

While inside Blossom Reach, **crouch and use the locket** to receive coordinates for nearby Blush Reef water. Swim down to see the living coral, luminous Rose Nacre, fish and Ribbon Dolphins. Aquatic Pokémon use Cobblemon's normal player spawner. Allow time around deep open water for a school or Pokémon to arrive.

## Pantry finds

Search the dimension's Lootr chests for Peach Blossom Mochi, Strawberry Cream Cake, Heart Cookie, Strawberry Milk, Custard Bun, Pink Starfruit and four jewel-toned berries, plus Petal Jam and Fairy Dew. These use normal Minecraft food and hunger rules. Rare keepsakes include a Blossom Lantern Charm, Petal Parasol, Shrine Bell Charm and Seashell Picnic Basket. The pantry items have their own pixel textures and do not add weapon recipes.

In the lower galleries, follow the lit walkway to the Mirror Court and ring its bell. Velvet Vesper has 500 health. Watch its boss bar and step away from its aimed needle and pollen attacks. Strike while its mirror is open. Hitting the raised mirror charms the attacker for 200 game ticks (10 seconds at normal speed), including when firing a projectile. Other players keep their own independent status. Ringing an occupied court cannot summon a second boss.

The installed Reactive Music songpack identifies the new mainland by its actual Minecraft cherry-grove biome and makes *Pinwheel* eligible through the floral rule. Its underwater rule makes *Distant Shores* eligible while submerged. Its special nighttime cherry-grove rule for *River Night* also requires the Overworld. Track selection still follows the pack's priorities and playback timing.

## Heartsill floors and rugs

Look for furnishings from Another Furniture in newly generated Japanese houses, the bathhouse, and the archive. Step into a house and look down the center aisle. Petal homes frame the original peach heart and lavender border in white and magenta, while Dusk homes pair it with black and purple. The tea shelf uses a matching slab, and a thin heart rug leaves the main aisle clear.

Heartsill comes in all 16 Minecraft dye colors, with a full tile, slab and carpet in each. For the pink original, place pink dye in the center of the crafting grid and one calcite above, below, left and right to make four blocks. Substitute another dye for its matching color. A horizontal row of three matching tiles makes six slabs. Place two matching wool blocks above a matching Heartsill block as shown in the recipe book to make four rugs. Upper and lower slabs combine into a double slab. Rugs need a supporting surface.

## Edit and build

The source project includes the Gradle wrapper. With a Java 21 JDK, run `./gradlew build` or `gradlew.bat build`. The playable JAR appears in `build/libs`. To run the dependency-matched development client, run `bash ci/get-mods.sh` from the project root, then `./gradlew runClient`. The download script verifies these JARs against checksums from the CurseForge profile.

The [hosted expedition workflow](.github/workflows/expedition.yml) builds the distributable, launches a separate packaged NeoForge server with dependency checksums matched to the CurseForge profile, and launches a graphical Minecraft client on a GitHub-hosted Linux runner. Test code lives in `ci/probe` and ships separately from the playable JAR.
